## Visual identity
Tokens: colors, typography, spacing, logo rules. Contrast requirements.

### Colors
| Token | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `primary` | #2B332A |Headings, key elements, brand surfaces |
| `secondary` | #2CFF05 |Accents and highlights only |
| `text` | #1C1C1C |Body text |
| `background` | #FFFFFF | Default background |
| `surface` | #2CFF05 | Secondary backgrounds, boxes |

Rules:
- Color balance per artifact: ~60% background/surface, ~30% primary, max 10% secondary.
- Do not create new colors, tints or gradients unless listed here.

### Typography
Roles and hierarchy apply everywhere; sizes are set per format.

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Relative size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Special Gothic SemiCondensed | Regular | 3× body |
| Heading | Special Gothic SemiCondensed | Regular | 1.75× body |
| Subheading | Special Gothic SemiCondensed | Regular | 1.25× body |
| Body | Special Gothic SemiCondensed | Regular | 1× |
| Caption | Special Gothic SemiCondensed | Regular | 0.8× body |

Fallback when the brand typeface is unavailable:
| Environment | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Microsoft Office | Arial |
| Google Workspace | Arial |
| Web | Arial, sans-serif |

Rules:
- Max two weights per artifact.
- Sentence case in headings. Never ALL CAPS, except short labels.
- Left-aligned text. Never justified or centered body text.

- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds.
- Never recreate or redraw the logo.

### Layout principles
- Generous white space. When in doubt, add space rather than remove it.
- One clear focal point per page, slide or view.
- Consistent margins: minimum 1/12 of the format's shortest side.
- Align elements to a grid; avoid free placement.
